Howdy! I’m rene; an artist in love with the world around me, and current undergrad neuroscience researcher interested in visual semantics and the subcortical systems governing image saliency that produce specific response behaviour from visual processing.

I’m from the great big semi-arid plains of Texas, enjoy a crisp tall lone star, reading fiction and non-fiction alike, and listening to music through my hi-fi headphones. You can usually find me drafting at my desk, behind my easel, on my couch, or various places in the great outdoors up to all kinds of silly things.
