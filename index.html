Howdy! I’m rene; a current undergrad researcher interested in visual semantics and the subcortical systems governing image saliency that produce specific response behaviour from epistemic encoding.

I’m from the great big semi-arid plains of Texas, and before getting into generalized neuro research, I had a full time studio practice at School of Visual Arts where the great professor Marvin Mattelson introduced me to the clinical science of portrait painters like Rembrandt and Jean Dominique Ingres. You can usually find me drafting at my desk, behind my easel,listening to music, or reading on my couch.
