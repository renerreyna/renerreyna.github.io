Keto, 2024
13 in x 17 in, Oil on Canvas

One day a neighbor saw me with my easel, doing plein air on the roof of my apartment building. He said he thought what I was making was awesome, and I invited him to sit for me sometime and I could paint his portrait from life. He was so excited by the idea he agreed to do it right then and there! He is also a Spurs fan, a rarity in New York.

Untitled, 2024
18 in x 24 in, Colored Pencil on Paper

I think this is my favorite of the set of a few here. It was perfectly captured, if you wanted to see what I saw, here it is, rendered as my brain did for me. I once read this passage that when someone makes art, even after they die, that persons brain is there. With michelangelo, rembrandt, this is a reflection of how they understood and saw the world, and put it into practice, as they saw fit to their vision. This is my interpretation of that cool, maybe slightly blushed in the cold air skin? focus on the pillar-like form in front of me, and profile as I could best describe it to myself.

Train, 2024
13 in x 27 in, Oil on Canvas

This one is from a view I had when I was granted the privelege of visiting China This is from a bullet train that was from shangai to Hangzhou, and I remember the hills and clouds rolling over each other just above them being really tranquil. I see why chinese artists for so long have drawn aesthetic inspiration from the graces of nature and subtle elegance in something so simple as a distant hill meeting the sky above it.
