Synthesis, 2026
576 px × 576 px, Digital drawing (Krita)

Here is a short blurb about this work. It’s envisioned as the glow that comes from art. That element beyond the canvas, that speaks beyond words. The red string theory some speak of, not to be blasphemous, but some also say this is how god speaks, or god himself. Whatever it is, or how you define it, there’s an unspeakable quality to it, and I consider it good.

Gradient, 2026

While not a true gradient, I think it reflects the virtue it serves: It sits at the top of my room to subtly define the darkness at the top from the lightness closer to where I "live" so that the visual focus and general value structure sits below an unnecessary point.

House, 2026
2.5 in x 4 in, Graphite on collaged sketchbook

I drew this from the first floor of a bar across the harbor there in Sydney. I asked if I could sit on the second floor, where a fence didn't obstruct my view, but it was closed for a newscast or somethig. They ended up setting me up where I could draw from a window in a bar without the fence in front of it. Strangers took photos of me, asked me questions, and indulged me in all the other delights of making art in public.
